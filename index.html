Inclusions (the “jardin”)

Emeralds are almost always included. Those internal fractures and wisps are so common that gem people call them the stone's garden, and a perfectly clean one is the unusual case.

Hardness is scratch resistance, not toughness. Emerald scratches little, but all those internal fractures mean it can still chip or crack from a knock, which is why it's usually set in protective mounts.
